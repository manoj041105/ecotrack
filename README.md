# 🌿 EcoTrack — Carbon Footprint Tracker

EcoTrack is a simple, front-end web app that helps you estimate, log, and reduce your personal carbon footprint. It breaks your emissions down by category, offers actionable tips, tracks your progress with streaks and badges, and compares your footprint against national and global benchmarks.

**🔗 Live demo:** [manoj041105.github.io/ecotrack](https://manoj041105.github.io/ecotrack/)

---

## ✨ Features

- **📊 Dashboard** — At-a-glance view of your annual carbon footprint (in tonnes of CO₂), broken down by:
  - 🚗 Transport
  - ⚡ Home Energy
  - 🍽️ Food & Diet
  - 🛍️ Lifestyle & Shopping
- **🧮 Calculator** — Answer a short questionnaire about your travel, energy use, diet, and shopping habits to get a personalized footprint estimate based on standard emission factors.
- **📋 Activity Log** — Log individual activities (transport, flights, energy use, food, shopping, recycling, green actions) with CO₂ impact, date, and notes.
- **💡 Insights** — Personalized recommendations to cut emissions, e.g. plant-based meals, cycling short trips, LED bulbs, buying second-hand.
- **📈 Benchmarks** — See how your footprint compares to India's average, the world average, and the Paris Agreement target.
- **🎯 Goals & Streaks** — Track weekly goals (e.g. meatless meals, low electricity use) and keep an eco streak going.
- **🏅 Badges** — Earn badges like *First Step*, *Cyclist*, *Recycler*, *No-fly Month*, *Vegan Week*, and *Solar Switch*.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites
Just a modern web browser — no build tools required.

### Run locally

```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/manoj041105/ecotrack.git

# Move into the project directory
cd ecotrack

# Open index.html in your browser
# (or use a local server, e.g.)
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then visit `http://localhost:8000` in your browser.
